# Duta Integra — SME & Rural Startups Layout & Tier Proposal

## 1. Overview
This redesign tailors Duta Integra's marketing proposition for **rural/suburban SMEs** (auto workshops, retail, F&B, clinics, wholesalers) and **early-stage non-technical startups**.

### Key Deliverables in this Update:
- **`Web/pks.html` & `Web/sme.html`**: Fully standalone, responsive bilingual landing page with:
  - Zero-jargon copywriting (plain Bahasa Melayu & conversational English).
  - Prominent WhatsApp 1-Click Message Generator with pre-filled business & package templates.
  - Interactive Lead Leakage & ROI Calculator.
  - Realistic local Malaysian SME case studies (Perak auto workshop, Selangor food distributor, Cyberjaya logistics startup).
  - Clear 3-tier service restructuring: **CORE**, **ADVANCE**, **PRO**.
