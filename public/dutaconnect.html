Process

How the pilot runs

Four steps from a WhatsApp message to a number you can act on. Today a person, not an agent, does the sending.

1

Capture

Enquiries from your site, the free check and your WhatsApp number are recorded in one lead pipeline, each tagged with the funnel that produced it.

2

Triage

Our team — with you — ranks the queue by what the enquiry actually says: budget, urgency, deal size. Automated scoring is on the roadmap; nothing is scored by a model yet.

3

Follow up

Replies are drafted for your number and go out only after a person approves them. Open and answered times are recorded against the lead.

4

Prove

At day 30 you get the baseline versus the pilot period from the stored leads: enquiries captured, replied to, and how many turned into meetings or quotes.

Safety

The guardrails

What we hold to today, and what is still being built. A person approves anything that reaches a prospect.

Human Control

Nothing is sent to a prospect without a person approving it. That is how the pilot runs today, and it stays true when the agents arrive.

One Account, One Console

Your enquiries live in one Postgres database with Row Level Security enabled, and only our console for your account reads them. Per-tenant isolation probes are roadmap — during the pilot there is one tenant.

Lead Trail

Every enquiry, the funnel that produced it, its status changes and the date it was replied to are stored and exportable to CSV from the lead pipeline. An append-only audit of every AI action is roadmap.

PDPA in Practice

During the pilot no prospect message is sent to a third-party model — the work is done by people on our console. Automated PII detection and forced local inference are roadmap.

Escalation Rules

High-value enquiries, anything you flag as sensitive and any lead gone quiet past your own cut-off come to a named person — during the pilot, that person is us, telling you.

Stop Anytime

There is no autonomous sender to pause today, and no lock-in: stop the pilot and nothing more goes out to your prospects. Per-agent pause switches are roadmap.

FAQ

Common questions

Which channels and systems does it connect to?

WhatsApp Business is the primary channel. Today enquiries reach us through the site forms, the free check and your own number, and are worked from one pipeline; replies are sent by a person from the WhatsApp Business app. An inbound signed webhook, and event pushes from n8n, ERPNext or Odoo, are roadmap. Email and CRM sync (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive) are roadmap too.

Does the AI message prospects on its own?

No — today nothing sends on its own. During the pilot a person drafts and sends every reply after you approve it. Scheduled follow-ups at +4h and +52h, a three-attempt cap and automatic escalation of high-value silence are the automation we intend to build, once server-side roles and an approvals queue exist to keep it safe.

How does lead scoring work?

In the pilot it is a rulebook, not a model: we agree what counts as hot for your business — budget, urgency, deal size, fit, engagement — and the queue is worked in that order, with the reason written on the lead. The five-signal extraction and a 0–100 score (HOT 80–100, WARM 60–79, COOL 40–59, COLD 0–39) with editable weights and bands are roadmap.

Can we customize the follow-up rules?

Yes, in the pilot: timing, attempt limits, escalation thresholds, language (Bahasa Melayu or English) and approval limits are set with us and applied to your queue by hand. Per-tenant configuration screens are roadmap.

Is prospect data secure?

Your leads sit in one Postgres database with Row Level Security enabled on the table, writes go through our server and only the admin console can read the queue. During the pilot no message content is passed to a third-party model, so nothing crosses a border for inference. Per-tenant isolation with a published verification probe, and automatic PII routing to local inference, are roadmap.
